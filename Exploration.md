# Purpose

[[Pillars]] are the game's pillars and control everything else. [[Discoverability]] makes sure there is *some* way for players to find every important game object. Exploration goes straight to level design: building places to maximize the feeling of discovery and exploration. This file explains the philosophy first, then makes specific guidelines.

# Philosophy

**Explore everywhere: the canopy, the sky, and the sea.** Climbing and leaping through the trees, rising to the sky, and diving into a dark [[Sea]] with air paths, gems to gather and ruins to explore.

**Movement is the differentiator.** Climbing and leaping set Canopy apart ([[Movement]]), so the route to a place is the main puzzle.

**Mapless.** There is no map. Markers are in the world: sparkles for gems, runestones, rings and wilters, and skybeams for firefly [[Cage|cages]]. A [[Tablet]] could hint at how to unlock a hidden spot, as [[Seeker Idol|Seeker Idols]] already do. [[Lookout|Lookouts]] let players spot sparkles.

**The player knows the kind of target, from its marker.**

- A ring: all rings are the same, with a ring-shaped sparkle.
- A [[Gem]]: a random sparkle, which tells its color. Gems share colors, so the color never says for sure which gem it is.
- A [[Runestone]]: its sparkle should be distinct from a gem's (todo).
- In [[Discoverability]]: Give runestones a sparkle distinct from gems' (Ken, 2026-10-31)

So far the player always knows basically what they are going for. For gems and runestones they don't know *which* variant they will get.

**Victory is felt at once.** Getting a ring, gem or runestone tells you immediately what it is (its Hoopla), so you feel the victory; otherwise you would have to haul it back to the [[Temple]] to find out. The locus is for discovering it; the Temple is for putting it into loadout.

**Encounters defend important places.** They are centered on the harvesters' defense of the places that matter. Dormant forms: [[Pod]] delivery, pop-ups and pop-outs (to be built), and simply getting close enough to see the setup of enemies around the challenge.

**Preview what's good.** Skyrim previews something good in abundance. That needs to be woven in, and not just seen from a distance.

**Value decides what is marked.** There is unmarked content, but all of it low value. The fear that players will miss gems or devices drives toward easy discoverability; leaving more unmarked might be better, but it is risky.

**Not too much at once.** A lookout that reveals too much, or a newly raised biome rife with skybeams and sparkles, may leave the player overwhelmed. Pods are part of solving that: only 2 to 5 firefly cages visible at a time, limiting choices.

# The loop, and its reveals

Reveals that bring the player delight or trepidation are marked.

1. **Lookout.** Lookouts are quite high up. The player mostly learns where an interesting treasure is. **Delight**, unless it shows so much that it overwhelms.
2. **Marker.** Its shape tells ring, gem or runestone, and a gem's sparkle its color. *Option:* all distant sparkles white, getting their color only once you are close. **Delight**
3. **Route.** Finding a way there by climbing and leaping. **Delight**
4. **Preview.** Seeing something good on the way, woven in, not only from a distance. **Delight**
5. **The defense.** Approaching reveals the setup of enemies around the challenge; perches to see it from already exist, organically. Sometimes only a hint of the defense is revealed, because a pod or pop-ups might be there, and only the player's approach will activate them. **Trepidation**
6. **Dormant forms wake.** Pop-ups open; a waiting pod releases its enemies. [[Wind Turbine|Wind turbines]] and [[Repairer|repair bots]] might aid this feeling. **Trepidation**
7. **Some don't wake.** Some pop-up lids are too rusted to open, or one opens and the platform rises, but empty. **Delight**
8. **Victory.** The Hoopla tells you at once what you got. **Delight**
9. **The Temple.** Putting it into loadout, later.
10. **Onward.** From there, the next target. This may imply the [[Outpost]] gets a way to quickly reach the Lookout above. **Delight**

**Delight:**

- A lookout's view of the sparkles.
- Gems and sparkles blazing through the sea's murk: it adds intrigue and a motive to dive.
- The world changing: the [[Sky Isle]]'s cloud shrinking away, the [[Shambles]] rising from the sea, a [[Shaman]]'s golden rays.
- A hint paying off: a hidden spot unlocked.
- Opening a [[Chest]].
- Some enemy defeats should prompt the reveal of treasure.
- The Hoopla.

**Trepidation:**

- Seeing the defense, or only a hint of it.
- A pop-up lid, or a pod waiting overhead.
- The [[Smauger]] hiding all its surrounding enemies in dense poisonous fog.
- The sea, so murky you must get close to see the enemy.

# Realms

**Canopy.** Spindly trees can loop routes back a little; the [[Tangle]] cannot really.

**Sky.** The [[Sky Isle]] and other places reached high up.

**Sea.** Dark and murky: gems and sparkles blaze through it, enemies are seen only up close, air paths let you go deeper, and ruins wait to be explored.

# Encounters

Encounters are the harvesters' defense of important places. Their dormant forms:

- **Pod delivery.** A [[Pod]] descends and delivers enemies.
- **Waiting pods.** Pods could descend and not immediately deliver their payload of enemies, waiting instead on the player's approach. To prototype.
- **Pop-ups.** A mechanized, harvester-style lid opens, and the harvester rises out of it. Some lids are just too rusted to open, or open and the platform rises, but empty. To prototype.
- **Fog.** The [[Smauger]] hides all its surrounding enemies in dense poisonous fog.
- **Murk.** In the sea you must get close to see the enemy.
